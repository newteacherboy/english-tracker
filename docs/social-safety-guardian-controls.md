# Social reporting and guardian controls — 18 October 2026

Existing students retain all social permissions by default. Neither requesting guardian verification nor setting a PIN disables participation. There is no waiting-for-approval gate. Courses, learning routes, individual games, progress and rewards retain their current rules. This opt-out design is a product requirement; it is not a claim that all child-safety policy obligations have been satisfied.

Profile → Veli Kontrolü sends a single-use link to the registered contact on first enrollment. After enrollment, recovery remains bound to the independently stored guardian address even if the student edits their account contact. The link holder declares they are an adult/legal guardian and creates a separate six-digit PIN. Email possession is verified; identity, age and legal guardianship are not established through government documents. Teachers are not automatically guardians. Links expire in one hour, PIN attempts are throttled, PINs are salted PBKDF2 hashes, parent sessions last ten minutes and are never persisted in browser storage. A PIN reset consumes outstanding links and invalidates old guardian sessions. Explicit lock revokes the server session.

Guardians manage social access, public visibility, peer preset messages, duels/invitations and gifts. APIs re-check restrictions and both sides of a peer interaction. Database triggers prevent restricted messages, gifts and match updates; automatic feed insertion is suppressed without failing the learning transaction. Public social lists, feed, presence and score/league responses filter restricted profiles. Block/report remain available independently of the social participation switch. School/teacher education messages remain separate from peer-social controls.

Profile/feed ellipsis menus and peer notification menus create reports with server-derived content snapshots and authenticated reporter IDs. Reporter identity is visible only to authorized administrators, never to reported users. Reporters see only status/reason in their own history. A request ID deduplicates submissions. Reports never automatically suspend users based on counts. Administrators can mark review, dismiss, warn, hide a feed event, suspend social features for one/seven days or lift an administrative suspension. Guardian restrictions survive lifting a suspension. Review actions are audited. A hidden feed event remains excluded from subsequent feed reads.

Private service-only tables have RLS and no anonymous/authenticated grants. Students cannot modify guardian state through their ordinary session or game-state JSON. Private safety data is deleted with its student account. Expired guardian sessions are cleaned hourly; challenges after expiry plus one day; resolved/dismissed reports and their review logs after ninety days. Open reports remain until resolved.

No AI chat feature is introduced or activated by this change.

Validation: 87 focused JavaScript regression tests, including eight DOM interaction scenarios. `tests/social-safety.sql` verifies controls, report ownership, idempotence, admin authorization, hiding, one-use links, forged/expired parent sessions, message enforcement, learning continuity, blocking and RLS inside a rolled-back database transaction. No real account was restricted or reported during verification.
