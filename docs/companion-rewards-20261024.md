# Companion completion rewards, 24 October 2026

Daily plan: 120 XP and 50 gold once per Istanbul calendar day. Each distinct supplementary story or dialogue: 50 XP, 20 gold and exactly 2 energy on its first completion. Replays retain their learning records without another reward. All three published questions must have server-recorded answers. The plan requires an actual server-recorded route completion, a completed story/dialogue today, and either no due review words or at least three words reviewed today. A client planner checkbox is not proof. No accuracy threshold is imposed on supplementary practice completion.

The server-only dm_companion_reward RPC locks the account and wallet, checks evidence, records a durable reserved extra_data marker and updates the wallet/energy in the same transaction. Public/anon/authenticated cannot execute it; only the authenticated API service can call it. Amounts and actor are server-owned.
